Delete your Oldo account

This page is the web resource for requesting deletion of an Oldo app account (com.oldo.app). Deletion is completed inside the app after you sign in with the same Google account. There is no separate web login that deletes the account by itself.

Delete in the app (required path)

  1. Install or open Oldo on your Android device.
  2. Sign in with the Google account used for Oldo.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

When you confirm, Oldo deletes your account access and associated app data as described below. You should see the sign-in screen afterward.

What deletion does

This deletes your account, your moods, and your messages. Reports you filed or that were filed about you may be kept for up to 90 days for safety reasons. You can sign in with Google again to create a new account.

  • Account is marked deleted; you cannot keep using that user id.
  • Mood text is scrubbed; embeddings cleared.
  • Your chat message bodies are scrubbed.
  • Push tokens for that account are removed.
  • Sessions are revoked; Google identity is unlinked from that user.

More detail: Privacy Policy.

If you already uninstalled Oldo

Reinstall Oldo from the same distribution you used before (Play closed testing or the install link you were given), sign in with the same Google account, then use Settings → Delete account. After deletion you may uninstall again.

Oldo does not currently publish a support email or web form that deletes accounts without signing into the app. If Google Play later requires an additional off-app request channel for production listing, this page will be updated.

Sign-out is not deletion

Signing out only clears the session on your device. Your account and data remain until you use Delete account.
